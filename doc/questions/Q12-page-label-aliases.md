# Q12 — Are label-named aliases for pages wanted, or clutter?

Source: RFC 0003 §9 question 6.
Status: **open** — answered when `A12-page-label-aliases.md` exists beside this file.

## Why it needs the owner

The labels are what a reader sees on the page, and ordinals are not.

## What the tree does meanwhile

Not built. Pages are ordinals only.

## Recommendation

Leave it out. Labels are not unique in real documents, so the directory needs a collision rule, and that is a design for a demand nobody has stated.
