# Q15 — Is PDF/A in scope at all, and which half: validator, converter, or both?

Source: RFC 0006 §10 question 1, and the owner's commission, which asked for a converter.
Status: **open** — answered when `A15-pdf-a-scope.md` exists beside this file.

## Why it needs the owner

A validator is a reader whose every answer is a citation, over machinery this tree already has. A converter is a producer whose hardest requirements are unsatisfiable, undecidable, or fenced by the authoring exclusion.

## What the tree does meanwhile

Neither is built. RFC 0006 is a draft.

## Recommendation

Build the validator, and decide the converter afterwards on a measurement rather than on the RFC's argument.
