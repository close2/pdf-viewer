# Q03 — Is Annex F declined permanently, or deferred?

Source: RFC 0002 §13 question 5, and `CLAUDE.md`'s amended exclusion, which says Annex F stays excluded until linearisation is separately ratified.
Status: **open** — answered when `A03-linearisation.md` exists beside this file.

## Why it needs the owner

The serializer could emit it; the exclusion says it may not until the owner says so. Leaving the word until in the file without a decision is how an exclusion decays into an assumption.

## What the tree does meanwhile

`optimize --linearize` is a usage refusal quoting that sentence.

## Recommendation

Decline permanently unless a corpus need appears. It is a producer's optimisation for a delivery model this viewer does not have.
