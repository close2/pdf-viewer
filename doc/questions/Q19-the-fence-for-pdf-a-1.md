# Q19 — Does the no-invented-marks fence move for part 1's transparency ban?

Source: RFC 0006 §10 question 5.
Status: **open** — answered when `A19-the-fence-for-pdf-a-1.md` exists beside this file.

## Why it needs the owner

Converting a transparent document to part 1 requires flattening, which replaces the producer's marks with a rendering of them, and that is exactly what the amended exclusion excludes.

## What the tree does meanwhile

Nothing built; the exclusion stands.

## Recommendation

Do not move the fence. Parts 2, 3 and 4 permit transparency, so refusing part 1 by name and pointing at part 2 is the better answer.
