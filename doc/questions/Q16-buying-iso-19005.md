# Q16 — Should the project buy ISO 19005, and which parts?

Source: RFC 0006 §10 question 2, and its §0, which says exactly what that round could and could not read.
Status: **open** — answered when `A16-buying-iso-19005.md` exists beside this file.

## Why it needs the owner

All four parts are paywalled. The RFC marks every requirement it could not read as second-hand, and principle 5 forbids implementing from a secondary source.

## What the tree does meanwhile

Nothing is implemented from the unread text, and the RFC says which of its own statements are evidence rather than clause.

## Recommendation

Buy parts 1 to 3 and wait on part 4's second edition, which is in ballot. Without them this feature cannot be built the way this project builds things.
