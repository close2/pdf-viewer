# Q254 — Does the owner accept RFC 0009, and how does it answer its nine questions?

**The question.** `doc/rfc/0009-what-each-exclusion-protects-and-what-lifting-it-would-mean.md` is
the RFC the owner commissioned on 2026-10-05 (*"please use one of the next rounds for a RFC for
features we have excluded until now and what it would mean, to implement them now (I am thinking
about printing, multimedia,...)"*). Its section 11 puts nine numbered questions, each with a
recommendation; this file is the one `Q` the round's block holds, and it asks the owner to answer
them **by number**, in one `A254`, the way `A54`–`A60` answered RFC 0007's. The nine, in one line
each:

1. whether RFC 0004 is finished in the order section 2.12 gives, with its three open questions
   closed as recommended — the DPI clamp kept, the spool container read as a transport envelope on
   the near side of the authoring line, IPP before the portal for the winit window;
2. whether clause 13's exclusion is amended so that it excludes the *player* and admits the *page*
   — the stream-form poster, every producer-supplied appearance, §13.2.2's viability computed and
   reported, the media file saved or handed to this machine's own player under a level — with the
   sentence section 11 writes, naming the two triggers that would reopen audio/video and 3D;
3. whether the hand-off's level is `Links` or a `Media` level of its own;
4. whether this program playing §13.3's PCM and µ-law sound objects (Tier B) is built now or on
   demand;
5. whether XFA stays excluded on two grounds — §K.1's permission and the authoring exclusion — with
   a per-document sentence to the reader built;
6. whether Route A — the file handed to CUPS as a baked derivative — becomes a named print option
   after the IPP path exists;
7. whether the authoring exclusion stays as written for text stamps, headers, footers, page
   numbers, a layout engine and HTML to PDF, and whether an overlay of a *producer's* page at a
   person's position (step 1) is wanted at all;
8. whether §10.6 stays `inapplicable` on a print path, with its one flipping route named in the row;
9. whether a CMYK spool through the output intent's BToA tables is wanted.

**Why it cannot be settled without the owner.** Questions 2, 5 and 7 change the text of
`CLAUDE.md`'s closed exclusion list, which the file says is revisited *by argument, never by
attrition*; question 1 closes RFC 0004's open scope sentence about a file this program writes;
question 3 adds a level a reader meets; `doc/rfc/README.md` makes the owner the decider of every
RFC's status.

**What the tree does meanwhile.** Exactly what it does today: two windows print and two show
print intent; every clause-13 action is refused by name and every excluded annotation without an
`/AP` is refused with its clause's sentence; §13.4's stream-form poster stays owed under
`doc/questions/A33` and is built whatever this answer is; a dynamic XFA document shows the page its
producer wrote for processors that decline; nothing composes a mark. No round starts section 12's
items 2–5 before `A254`.

**Recommendation.** Accept the RFC with its nine recommendations as written in section 11 — in one
line: finish RFC 0004 (winit panel and `ipp` over `ureq`, the confined spool writer, the docked
preview, tiling), the clamp kept and the spool on the near side; amend clause 13's entry to admit
the still page and exclude the player with its triggers named; a `Media` level of its own, `ask`
by default; Tier B on demand; XFA excluded on two grounds with the sentence built; Route A named
and never default; authoring steps 2 and 3 excluded and step 1 not unless qpdf parity is a stated
goal; §10.6 inapplicable with its route named; no CMYK spool.
