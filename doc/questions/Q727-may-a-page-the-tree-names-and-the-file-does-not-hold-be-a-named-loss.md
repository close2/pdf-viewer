# Q727 — May a page the tree names and the file does not hold be a named loss rather than a refusal?

Source: the merge of batch 127 (sessions 1861–1870), reading the PDF/A census against the base.

## The question

`A Defence of the New Tenseless Theory of Time.pdf` (format-corpus, `jhove-errors/PDF-HUL-35`) states
13 pages in its page tree root and 10 in one node, and that node holds nine pages and one kid whose
cross-reference offset points into a stream. The base converted it under both shipped profiles by
carrying what the reader drew. Round 1862's Table 30 rule (ADR 2558) now declines the kid that names no
dictionary, the rebuild under `recovered-structure` carries the 12 pages the reader reaches, and ADR
2502's rule refuses by name, because writing 12 pages where the root states 13 without saying so is a
loss nobody authorised. The refusal is right under the rules the owner has set; the document is one a
company could see and so wants archived (the owner's words of 2026-10-27).

Shall "a page the tree names that the file does not hold" be a named loss of its own — proposed word
`page-not-held`, the count the root states and the count carried written into the statement page's facts
— that a profile may authorise, so that the document converts under a profile accepting it and is
refused under one that does not? The five other documents the same rule newly refuses
(`T02-02_003_page-tree-different-kids`, `T02-03_006_page-object-missing` and three fuzzed files) each
hold a kid naming no dictionary; those need no new loss, only `recovered-structure`, which the shipped
`as-if-printed` and `as-shown` do not take. Should those two profiles take it for a kid that names no
page, since dropping such a kid changes nothing a reader shows?

## Why it cannot be settled without the owner

A new loss word is a term of the owner's "big table" of problems and remedies (RFC 0014), and whether
the shipped profiles accept a structural loss is the owner's choice of what each profile means.

## What the tree does meanwhile

The document is refused by name at both targets under both shipped profiles, with the sentence naming
the kid, the root's count and the count reached (ADR 2502). The five others are refused under the two
shipped profiles and convert under any profile that authorises `recovered-structure`.
