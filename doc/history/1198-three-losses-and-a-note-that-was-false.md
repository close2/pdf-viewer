# 1198 — Three losses the clause states the fallback for, and a note that was false

**The instrument first (ADR 1233).** `Configuration::unbuilt` counted a `discard` as not carried out
unless the loss table named the site. A site answered `Answer::Mechanical` is not in that table —
nothing about it needs authorising — nor in `--remedy-sites`' listing, which lists what refuses, so
four such answers were reported as owed *and* as at a site the listing does not name, the column
`tools/state.sh remedies` prints to catch exactly this. A `discard` says
*do not stop here*, and at a row that costs nothing that is already done.
`decision::answered_without_authorisation` is the second question `unbuilt` now asks; `Loses`,
`AsUnderlying` and ADR 1199's `CallerSuppliesTheBytes` stay named.

**Then three remedies (ADR 1234)**, each one the base standard states the fallback for:

- `annotations/printable-and-visible` → `--authorise hidden-annotation`. The annotation leaves its
  page's `/Annots`, its popup with it. §12.5.3's Table 167 offers no fallback for the flags, and the
  other future — writing the ones the clause asks for — puts a mark on a page its producer kept off.
- `annotations/appearance-dictionary-holds-only-normal` → `--authorise appearance-states`. `/AP` is
  reduced to `/N`; §12.5.5's Table 170 gives `/R` and `/D` the default *the value of the N entry*,
  which is also why a dictionary stating no `/N` is refused by name instead.
- `optional-content/no-automatic-states` → `--authorise automatic-states`, a part 2 target's alone.
  §8.11.4.1's Table 98 puts a configuration in three places; the removal reaches all three, editing
  what the `/Order` completion left rather than a source snapshot.

`pdf-archive` gained `annotations_the_flags_forbid`, `annotations_with_extra_appearance_states`,
`flags_permitted` and `flags_permitting` — the requirements' own predicates read as populations,
ADR 1099's rule. Round 1194's five escaped section signs, all genuine ISO 32000-2 clause numbers
written `\u{a7}` where the citation gate could not see them, are now `§`.

**Measured** (`tools/state.sh remedies`, before → after). Answers not carried out:
`only-metadata-loss` 7 → 5 at 2b/2u/2a, 5 → 4 at part 4; `as-if-printed` 15 → 8 at 2b/2u/2a, 15 → 10
at 4, 13 → 8 at 4f/4e; `keep-everything` 32 → 31 at 2b. The *at a site the listing does not name*
column is 0 everywhere but `keep-everything`. Sites not built: 57 → 54 at 2b, 62 → 59 at 2a, 52 → 50
at part 4. No profile is fully carried out: the XMP-packet family, the amendment identifier, the
forms sites and the information dictionary are what is left.

**Left on purpose.** `preserve` at the flag site — un-hiding, which `keep-everything` asks for —
needs a mechanism the vocabulary has not got: `preserve` carries a placement or a tool, and this one
keeps the annotation where it is; `flags_permitting` is the value it would write.
`optional-content/configuration-names` is the catalogue's `supply` with the converter as supplier,
which is its own argument.
