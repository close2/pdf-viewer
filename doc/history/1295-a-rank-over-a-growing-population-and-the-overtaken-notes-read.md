The instruments slot of batch forty-five. ADR 1427. Question Q192.

**Superlatives.** `tools/superlatives.py` (`tools/state.sh superlatives`) lists each sentence in
`oracle.rs`, `corpus.rs`, `raster_golden` and the ledger's notes that pairs a rank word with a scope
over a growing population. The count went from 117 to 104, and in `oracle.rs` from 45 to 32. There
were eighteen rewrites: the four "tightest this bucket/group has measured" claims that contradicted
issue12963 p1's 0.00003 and one another; `AMBIGUOUS_IMAGE_REDUCTION`'s "tightest agreement" and
"largest reduction", overtaken inside that note by issue4379's five decimals and issue1985's 43;
`CONTRADICTED_UNEXPLAINED`'s "largest single fall", 15, overtaken by the 17 one section down; "the
smallest rasters in the bucket", which issue1985's 20×21 undercuts; issue840 p2's claim over 0.0001;
160F's "a scale nothing else in the corpus reaches", which issue7454's 9 levels overtakes; and §9.3's
ledger note, "the longest this project has recorded". The ranks still true of their scope stay. The
ledger's 2967 and 5965 ranks were not verified; that needs a measurement, not a reading.

**The 37 overtaken notes.** Six were ADR 1415's appendix alone, a false positive; `overtaken::decided`
now reads an ADR above its appendix. The other 31 were read against their decisions and entered in
`READ`, and eight had sentences made false, which were corrected: `NO_RENDER_ENCRYPTION_…`
(issue21579 opens by ADR 1377; PDFBOX-4352's mechanism is `E<`), `REFUSED_OPEN`, `PAGELESS` (stale
upper half cut; issue19484's argument now beside its `INCOMPLETE` entry), `SELECTION_BELOW_FLOOR`,
`REFUSED_BY_THE_DEVICE(_AT_FOUR)`, `AMBIGUOUS_TRANSFER_FUNCTION_UNAPPLIED`,
`CONTRADICTED_IMAGE_SAMPLE_…` and `A_FLAT_SHEET`. The count went from 37 to 3, and the three left are the sibling's ADR 1419.
