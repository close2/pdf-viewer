# 1064 — What the twenty-one differing pages are made of

2026-09-15. No ADR: nothing here is a decision a later round must not re-litigate. The oracle's undiagnosed head was empty for a sixth round, so the contract was `render-raster`'s own differing list. Files: `crates/render-raster/examples/ink_ladder.rs` (new), `crates/render-raster/tests/corpus.rs` (`differing_pages`' comment), this file. Five siblings live in the tree.

The gate's three figures all compare pixel against pixel at one scale, where two unrelated faults read alike: the same ink in different places, and different amounts of ink. Only the second is something a clause arbitrates. `ink_ladder` separates them — each backend's total ink at 1×, 2×, 4× and 8×, scale-normalised, so the reference is the geometry the page states, not either backend.

**Eleven of the twenty-one agree within 3% at every rung**: nothing missing, nothing mis-sized, what is left is placement — the oracle's being the coarser. `tiny-skia` supersamples four times per pixel row and quantises a run to quarter-pixel steps along `x` (`SUPERSAMPLE_SHIFT = 2`), so `render-cpu` states every path edge on a quarter-pixel grid. Calibrated on the two-colour pages, 89–100% of its inked values lie within 1.5 levels of the resulting 1/16 lattice where raster sits at 11–20%, which is what chance gives; `endchar.pdf`'s four measurable edges are raster's fractional positions rounded to the nearest quarter, all four. §10.7.4: "Its coordinates are mapped into device space but not rounded to device pixel boundaries." **The wrong backend on those eleven is the CPU oracle**, and the fix is a scan converter rather than a page: ADR 0476, 0583 and 0226 each moved one shape class off that quantum, and what is left is the general path — which is what small text is.

**Ten part, and the shape of the gap says what each is.** `issue16473`, `issue19083`, `bug1844583`, `bug1978317`: raster 5–16% short at 1×, level by 4× — ADR 0355's clip-against-the-mark product, measured in ink rather than pixels for the first time; `doc/QUORRA_FEEDBACK.md` section 24 is the standing ask. `issue20232` and `issue21068` are that shape with the sign reversed, raster 32% and 3.1% long at 1× and halving each rung. `22060_A1_01_Plans` is the one gap that does **not** close (raster ~8% long, flat) — geometry rather than boundary, and the page worth opening next.

**`issue15150.pdf` convicts both**, and its whole content stream is `0.5 w 1 0 0 RG 0 9.75 m 0.5 9.75 l s`: the stroked region is the device rectangle `[0, 0.5] × [0, 0.5]`, a quarter of pixel (0, 0). Both draw that at 2× and above; at 1× `render-cpu` lays 0.1875 and raster 0.5. The oracle's is the side §10.7.4 forbids — "The area covered by painted pixels shall always be at least as large as the area of the original shape" — and the route is closed: `s` closes the subpath, `tiny-skia`'s stroker emits the outline as two contours with a collinear vertex per side on the inner, so `pdf_render::sub_pixel_bands` declines a path it would otherwise measure exactly and the rule falls to ADR 0268's widened band, whose ink off the top of the raster is lost. Not fixed here: the rectangle predicate and the contested-line test both have to move and the predicate is shared with `pdf_render::edge` — its own round, not a corpus slot's.
