That rung is the control. `QUORRA_FEEDBACK` §46 is the ask.

**The substitution question was real on the other nine, and the clause decides it by way of the device.** §10.7.4's restatement exists so that "no shape ever disappears", which under the anti-aliasing departure means a coverage that rounds to nothing — so it is owed where the raster cannot state the shape's coverage and nowhere else. ADR 0268 was written against a converter that could state none under a sixteenth and hairlined every stroke at or under a pixel; ADR 1082 replaced it with the exact winding integral, down to the raster's own eight bits. The boundary is `pdf_render::unmeasurable_width` — one *level* of a device pixel — and between that and one whole pixel a stroke is drawn as the shape the document states, which is what raster draws from the same stated width.

**Three candidates, measured on the nine before choosing.** (A) the widening at one whole pixel, as it was; (B) no substitution above one level, `sub_pixel_bands` included; (C) the closed form kept for an axis-aligned rectangle and only the widening moved. B and C differ on one page and C is the clause's answer there: `issue15150.pdf` reads **0.251** of pixel (0, 0) under C, the area itself, against 0.5 under B — its `s` closes a two-point subpath, so the outline is one rectangle traversed twice the same way round and only the closed form sees that.
