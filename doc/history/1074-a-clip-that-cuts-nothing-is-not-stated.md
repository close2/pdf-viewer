# 1074 — A clip that cuts nothing is not stated

2026-09-15. ADR 1088 is the decision. Files: `crates/render-raster/src/{scene.rs,stroke.rs}`, `crates/render-raster/tests/corpus.rs`, `crates/render-raster/tests/clip_that_cuts_nothing.rs` (new), `crates/render-raster/examples/clip_cost.rs` (new), `doc/QUORRA_FEEDBACK.md` §24c and §45, §10.7.4's ledger row, this file. Five siblings live in the tree.

**The sixteen re-classified from the run, not from 1064's table.** `ink_ladder` over the whole differing list: nine are within ~2% at every rung and are placement (`issue11473`, `issue12295`, `issue16038`, `issue18030`, `issue269_2`, `issue2177`, `issue4402_reduced`, `pr12564`, `standard_fonts` — the last two heavy on *both* backends at 1×, which is 8 pt text under §10.7.4's "at least as large"). `22060_A1_01_Plans` is the one gap that does not close. That left two classes of three, both raster's and both the same size: **raster short and closing** — `issue16473` −16.5%, `bug1844583` −11.8%, `issue19083` −11.5% — and **raster long and closing** — `issue20232` +31.3%, `issue21068` +3.1%, `issue15150` ×2. `bug1978317` was on 1064's list and had left under 1068.

**The short class is a clip, and the licence in the clause had not been taken.** §10.7.4 makes the painted region "the intersection of the set of pixels defined by the clipping region with the set of pixels for the region to be painted", so an intersection with a region containing the mark *is the mark* — which means the clip need not be stated at all. raster multiplies the two coverages in `coverage.wgsl` (`cov * extent.x * extent.y`), which squares a mark's coverage where its own edge shares a pixel with its clip's; a containing clip costs nothing only if it is left off. `Encoder::cuts_nothing` asks that per mark, in **page** space so the answer is the document's and not the view's (no `consume_view`, ADR 0702 intact), against the rectangle the chain admits — an *inner* bound, the opposite of `DisplayList::clip_bounds`. Held back: a chain with a link that is not a rectangle, a fill with a collapsed subpath, and a stroke that made §8.5.3.2's dot, gave up width to §10.7.4, or whose width the device resolves to a whole pixel — each one device pixel across, a size the view decides. A group keeps its clip: §8.5.4 gives its result the union of its elements' shapes and nothing here bounds that.

**Measured with a new instrument** — `examples/clip_cost` draws a page twice per backend, as stated and with every clip off, so for a clip that cuts nothing the difference is the composition. At 1×: `issue16473` cpu 498.45 of 501.93 against raster **501.87 of 501.87**, `bug1844583` 486.46 of 501.76 against **500.93 of 500.93**. Both left the differing list.

**Two arrived, and they are the finding.** `bug1844576` reads cpu 849.31 of 933.38 against raster 933.09 of 933.09, and `bug1978317` cpu 13 387.41 of 15 165.68 against raster 15 118.43 of 15 118.43 — so those clips now cost the device nothing and cost the **oracle** 9.0% and 11.7% of the page, and cost it nothing at 8×, which is the signature of ADR 0268's widened mark meeting a clip by `min`. §10.7.4's third sentence ranks them and the oracle is under it. The direction is the device moving onto the geometry; re-stating the clip here to shorten the list would move it away. `render-cpu`'s round.

**`issue19083` is what is left of the clip class, and it is upstream's.** Its border is `0.5 0.5 124.2502 19 re s` at `1 w` in a `/BBox` of `[0 0 125.25 20]`, so the outline's outer edge lies 0.0002 *outside* the box: the clip genuinely cuts, the rule declines it by name, and raster's product then costs **11.4%** of the page (396.96 against 447.87 unclipped). `doc/QUORRA_FEEDBACK.md` §24c carries it with the table.

**The long class was diagnosed and handed upstream, not taken.** Reproduced without a document: a 0.75-wide band on an 8 × 4 page, stated twice with the same winding, reads 2.9961 on the oracle and **4.0000** on raster at 1× and at 2× alike; stated once, both read 2.9961; stated twice opposed, both read 0 (the control). §8.5.3.3 makes the painted region a *set* and raster clamps the winding **integral** per pixel, so a self-overlapping outline — a stroke's quads at a join, or the out-and-back `s` makes of a two-point subpath — reads over. It is ADR 1082's own defect on the other backend, §11.6.2's "[p]ortions of an object shall not be composited with one another". `doc/QUORRA_FEEDBACK.md` §45 is the ask.

**Trap 13 caught a defect in the test, not the tree.** The first draft of `clip_that_cuts_nothing.rs` used a *rectangular* mark and passed with the rule planted off: an axis-aligned rectangular fill takes the device's rectangle path, where a clip rectangle is met geometrically and never by a product. With a triangle the planted run reads raster 3.0353 at 1× and 3.2853 at 2× against an area of 4, where the rule on gives 3.9961 and 4.0069 — both backends. The file's second test holds the other side, that a clip which cuts still cuts.
