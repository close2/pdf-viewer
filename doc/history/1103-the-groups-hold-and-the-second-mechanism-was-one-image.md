## The one thing that was not the mechanism it said

`CONTRADICTED_GLYPH_EDGES` wrote of `pdfbox/unencrypted.pdf page 2` that its heatmap is "hollow letters *and* one-pixel edges around the yellow table cells", left that half unestablished, and named `CONTRADICTED_ANTIALIASED_EDGES` for it. It is neither the table nor that group. The page states **one** `/XObject` and draws it through `211.6685486 0 0 317.5028076 84.9489288 367.9589233 cm`, and every long run of differing pixels is an edge of it: left 84.95, right 296.62, top 106.54, bottom 424.04, which is where the runs are. Our band's left edge sits at **84.95** on every row and `poppler`'s, `mupdf`'s and `ghostscript`'s at **84.00–84.06**, widths agreeing to 0.05 of a pixel — the three round the image's origin onto the pixel grid, and §10.7.4 says a shape "is a path to be painted with the current colour or with an image" whose "coordinates are mapped into device space but not rounded to device pixel boundaries". The clause answers this half for us, as it answers `CONTRADICTED_IMAGE_SAMPLE_AT_THE_PIXEL_CENTRE`'s; that group is the same subclause's sampling sentences and this is its rounding one.

**The control is what divides the two mechanisms, and it moves a bound.** Masking the image's box out takes ours against `poppler` to ssim **0.9068** and against `mupdf` to **0.9058** — inside 0.9000, where the whole page is **0.8965** and outside it — so the structural bound this page alone of its group fails is the image's, while the differing fraction that convicts it stays outside (6.20% against 5.00%) and is the glyphs'. Over the same masked page the convicting pair differs on **0.0204%** of channels at ssim 0.99995: outside that image the two are one raster, which is what puts the bound on its floor. The page stays where it is; its note now says both mechanisms. **No rendering edit is owed** — the displacement is the references' rounding and not ours, so round 1099 has nothing from here.
