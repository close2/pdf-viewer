# 1943 — The assumed inks are an ICC profile, and every profile but `as-if-printed` states them

Session 1943, a single archive round. ADRs 2700, 2701; no question; A780's `Owes` cleared.

**The premise.** This reader draws `DeviceCMYK` through a CMYK intent's `A2B` two ways: a fill as
`ICCBased` over the intent, through the profile; a composite through the intent's press, the `A2B`
sampled at 17 an ink and interpolated in sRGB. A sampled `A2B` matches the cube at its nodes (0.26
levels, 16-bit XYZ) and not between them, since the cube is multilinear in encoded sRGB; the brief's
"read only at nodes" held for the press and failed for fills. Measured sides (ramps / 17⁴ grid /
cell midpoints, levels): 9 → 4.6/4.5/4.0; 17 → 1.16/0.26/1.30; **21 → 0.88/0.79/0.78**; 33 →
0.36/0.26/0.26 at 7.3 MB. Over `Lab ` sides 2, 3, 5 miss the ramps by 48.8, 28.0, 12.9.

**The construction (ADR 2700).** `pdf_colour::colour::assumed_inks_profile`: ICC.1:1998-09 v2.2.0,
`prtr`, `CMYK` over `XYZ `, the ten tags of Table 29, one `lut16Type` per direction shared by the
three intents. `A2B` 21⁴; `B2A` 33³ over encoded sRGB (section 6.5.6's XYZ matrix to linear sRGB,
4096-entry encoding tables) sampling `rgb_to_ink`. Over `Lab ` the same `B2A` round-tripped the cube to
25.0 levels; over sRGB **3.62**, 3 740 of 83 521 past one. Fixed date, `f64` powers; checked in as
`data/icc/quorra-assumed-inks.icc` (1.6 MB), regenerated by an example and compared by a test.

**The routing (ADR 2701).** CMYK intent = the assumed inks unless `press-assumed` is authorised,
then GRACoL with ADR 2691's sentence; the loss is now asked at every target, so `as-if-printed` keeps
GRACoL at 2b too. Its sentence keeps "already showed those colours that way" and adds that
transparency now blends in the profile's components and that it is not a physical press.

**Transparency.** Six of the 30 changed documents (soft masks, blend modes, `DeviceCMYK` groups)
still differ by up to 25 levels (GRACoL: 53): identical with the intent renamed, and identical with
the conversion into the press by search instead of `B2A`, so the intent's blending space, not the
profile. The other 24 draw within one level at both parts (`postscript_type4_many_outputs` 76 → 1).
