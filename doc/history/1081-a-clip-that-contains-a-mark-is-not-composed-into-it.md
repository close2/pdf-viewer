# 1081 — A clip that contains a mark is not composed into it

2026-09-15. ADR 1095 is the decision. Files: `crates/render-cpu/src/{lib,scan}.rs`, `crates/pdf-render/src/{sub_pixel,display_list}.rs`, `crates/render-cpu/tests/clip_that_cuts_nothing.rs` (new), `crates/render-raster/tests/corpus.rs` (the named list only), `crates/pdf-model/tests/raster_golden.tsv`, §10.7.4's ledger row, this file. Five siblings live in the tree.

**The contract's hypothesis was half right, and the measurement is what split it.** `examples/clip_cost` and a per-command ink diff on `bug1844576.pdf` put the whole 84.07 of the loss on **one** command — a `1 w` rectangle border whose outline is exactly its `/BBox` — and the losing pixels read `c²`, not `min(c, c)`. A probe said why: `pdf_render::thinnest_line` reads **1.0** on that page's appearance matrix and `pdf_render::band_substitute_width` reads **0.9999999**, the first through a square root and the second through a ratio of mapped lengths. `draw_sub_pixel_rule` admits a stroke at or under the first, hands the widening one at or under the second, and `draw_stroked_outline` declines the same width from the other side — so the mark fell past all three to `tiny_skia::PixmapMut::stroke_path`, which multiplies the clip in. `scan::stroke`'s doc list of what still arrives there was short by exactly this case, for eleven sessions.

**The floor recovered 72.71 of 84.07 and the residue was structural.** With `band_substitute_width` floored at `thinnest_line` — arithmetic, not caution: with `s1 >= s2`, `|T u| / |u|` is in `[s2, s1]` and `|det T|` is `s1 s2`, so every direction's answer is in `[1/s1, 1/s2]` — the mark reaches `scan::fill` and `min`. It still read 922.02 of 933.38, because a stroker's ring has a same-wound crossing at its close, `render_cpu::area` declines it (ADR 1082), and `tiny-skia` then states its boundary pixels **to the nearest quarter** where the clip rectangle beside it is measured by §10.7.4's own closed form (ADR 0476). `min` cuts the quarter that rounded up and does not restore the one that rounded down: mark 127, clip 112, fifteen levels a pixel over 340 boundary pixels.

**So the licence, and the half of it that is the decision.** `MaskCache::cuts_nothing` leaves a containing clip off the mark, in page space, against `DisplayList::clip_admits` — an *inner* bound, the opposite of `clip_bounds` — keeping the clip's band so that no command gains a page-tall buffer. The containment is asked about the shape the **document** states, not the widened one: `bug1978317.pdf` is 15 004 rules half a device pixel tall inside clips half a device pixel tall, and cutting ADR 0268's band back to the clip leaves three quarters of the ink, which "[t]he area covered by painted pixels shall always be at least as large as the area of the original shape" forbids. That is also what makes this the same rule raster took (ADR 1088), whose `reach` is the document's outline too.
