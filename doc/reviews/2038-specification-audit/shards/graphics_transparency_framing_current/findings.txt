# Graphics and transparency framing review

Baseline: `b901326c`. This is a bounded audit of the selected aggregate, introductory, and framing rows, not a re-review of every child requirement. I read the effective sponsored EC3 PDF at the defining pages and compared the claims with `doc/conformance/ledger.toml`, implementation comments/code, existing assertion bodies, and the prior owner shards. No tests were run and no tracked files were changed.

§8.4.1 has reader behavior beyond its initial-value tables: correct types/ranges, clipping numeric values when required, and retaining the clipped logical value without baking output-device capability adjustment back into state. The ledger row cites selected tests and tells a stronger aggregate story than those citations prove on their own. `line_parameters.rs` does directly assert selected defaults, line-width/miter clipping, and a paint-time minimum width, while `coordinates_graphics_state_current` and the scan-conversion shard cover selected rest-state/default facets. This is an evidence distribution/traceability limit, not a newly found runtime defect. It does not establish every Table 51/52 value or every setter's no-device-adjust-back path.

The §8.5, §8.7 and §8.9 introductions do not supersede their specialized clauses. I cross-referenced the existing path, tiling/shading, image/sample/filter, mask, and transparency shards instead of claiming a chapter pass. In particular, the prior `images_current` malformed-Decode concern remains there; this frame review does not reproduce it. Likewise, the confirmed B39 same-object alpha stroke case and the degenerate tiling BBox issue are already owned by the stroke and pattern shards.

§8.8.1/Table 86 gives the operative `Do` requirements: the name is in the current XObject resource subdictionary, its value is a stream, `/Type`, if present, is `/XObject`, and `/Subtype` chooses Image or Form. `content/run/xobject_ops.rs` routes the operator through `draw_xobject`, and `content/xobject.rs` resolves that current resource, requires a stream, and dispatches. I found no new valid-input dispatch defect in this bounded pass.

Table 94's `/Type` row in §8.10.3 is a precise producer-validity boundary: the entry is optional, and when present its value shall be `/Group`. The reader dispatch checks the group's required `/S` but does not gate output on `/Type`. The current inventory marks `group_type_if_present` unreviewed, but the table sentence alone does not direct a reader to reject a group with absent or wrong `/Type`; I therefore do not report this as a reader defect or draft a reader test. Tests in `form_xobjects_audit.rs` cover form execution, while group `/S /Transparency` behavior belongs to §11.4/§11.6.6.

§11.1 explicitly allows equivalent results from an implementation that differs from the illustrative implementation mechanics. §11.2 requires the transparency stack result to be determined by all overlapping elements, in bottom-to-top stacking order, with blend mode, shape and opacity; Note 1 says immediate rasterization or committing to intermediate raster output is unnecessary. The buffered group implementation and specialized CPU paths are therefore judged by their observable composites and restricted premises, not by whether they keep the specification's conceptual accumulators. Existing compositing/group shards provide those selected controls. §11.7.1 is only an introduction to the following colour/rendering interactions and has no independent SHALL.

The broad Clause 11 headings do not certify their children. The review map and source locators are in `features.json`; owner-shard evidence remains explicitly partial. EC3 #345 (physical 407–408 / printed 392–393) says Figures 72–73 are illustrative, not pixel- or colour-precise, so figures are not used as numerical oracles. #688 (physical 428 / printed 413) corrects two alpha symbols in the §11.4.8 summary and does not change the recurrence reviewed at §11.4.2. The two #619 carets on physical 436 relate to Table 143 / §11.6.5.2, not §11.6.6.

Selected ADR premise checks are in `decisions.json`. ADR 0046's historical statement that Clause 11 was complete is not new evidence for currently pending rows; its blend-mode test-design rationale is retained. ADR 0237's algebra applies to the restricted non-isolated/Normal branch and should not be described as a claim that the implementation must maintain separate general-form accumulators. ADR 0056's pattern BBox finding remains with its owner shard and its specific nondegenerate controls.

No new test draft was justified in this bounded pass. Explicit residuals: not all state-table values/setters have independent assertions here; no full §8.5/8.7/8.9/Clause 11 recertification; no re-run of child tests; no backend-wide verification; and no conversion of producer-side constraints into reader rejection rules.
