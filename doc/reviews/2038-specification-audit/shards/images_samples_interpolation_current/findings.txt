The exact-xref `image_coordinates_audit.rs` is a high-quality public render fixture for the §8.9.4 mapping: its 3×2 RGB sample bytes encode six distinct colors, and literal expected pixels discriminate row order, square placement despite a 3:2 source grid, reflection, and a skew transform. It checks a white medium outside the image, requires a complete interpretation, and asserts the document was not recovered. I found no reason to duplicate these green geometry assertions. The fixture demonstrates one valid combination of §8.9.2's four parameters; it does not certify all codecs or color-space families.

`image_interpolation_audit.rs` is also a valid exact-xref fixture with normal/reflected controls, absent-versus-false default comparison, literal source interiors, and seam pixels. It is useful regression coverage for this implementation's choice to honor `/Interpolate true` on magnified images. Its expected mixed grayscale/bilinear transition is not a normative oracle: §8.9.5.3 says true declares that interpolation might improve rendering, calls it a hint, and expressly allows a processor to ignore it. The implementation's native-scale branch and handling of reduction are separate decisions; reduction is §10.7.4 / ADR 0025 scope. Do not require a particular interpolation kernel or cross-backend identical pixels from this clause.
