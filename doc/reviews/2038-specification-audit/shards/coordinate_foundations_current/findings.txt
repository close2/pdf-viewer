Source checked: ISO 32000-2:2020 EC3 held primary PDF, physical pages 164–167 / printed 149–152. `pdftotext -layout` matched the actual headings: §8.3.1 and §8.3.2.1 on physical164; §8.3.2.2 on165; §§8.3.2.4–.5 on167. The visible PDF annotations on those pages are internal `/Link` navigation targets; no coordinate-specific editorial/errata annotation was found. The EC3 layout matches these passages. This review does not rely on the generated Markdown page-number mapping.

The clause inventory marks all these rows implemented, but the rows are broad and the aggregate status alone is not evidence. Their source obligations are largely foundational descriptions: coordinate systems establish origin, axis orientation and unit lengths; device space is output-dependent; and each specialized space has its own transformation relationship. The concrete implementation obligations are best tested at each consumer boundary.

For §8.3.2.1, the held text says paths and positions use Cartesian pairs of real numbers and identifies the three properties defining a space. `pdf-syntax` numbers enter content as f64, then `run::narrow` and the display-list geometry represent them as f32. Existing content-transform assertions use simple values, while fractional page geometry and text-advance assertions show non-integer positions are accepted. No source-defined precision tolerance establishes f32 as correct for all finite inputs, but the standard also does not require a particular internal precision or exact raster output. Keep large-coordinate / near-device-pixel rounding as an explicit representational residual; it is not a demonstrated defect.

§8.3.2.2 explains that device origin, axis direction and resolution vary with display/printer, and warns against encoding page objects in device coordinates. The product's `TargetSpec` is the output-device boundary; page interpretation keeps a device-independent page transform and rasterizes into the target scale. Existing page-size and image-pixel controls exercise output mapping. There is no fixed device origin/resolution to assert as an ISO requirement, so a test that expects every host device to share one would be misguided.

§8.3.2.4 is a list of mappings, not just a concept heading: text space goes through its text matrix and state; glyph space through a font matrix; images occupy a fixed 1×1 image-space square regardless of sample dimensions; forms and patterns use their matrices; and 3D coordinates are projected to an annotation target. Coverage is distributed across direct consumer tests: text_positioning_audit.rs and type3_spec_audit.rs for text/glyph; image_coordinates_audit.rs for a literal 3×2 sample grid occupying a transformed 1×1 square; form_xobjects_audit.rs for Form Matrix composition; tiling.rs for pattern matrix and form-invocation space. These are useful, source-separated controls. They are not an exhaustive test of every transform or input class. 3D is specifically left outside this audit.

§8.3.2.5's Figure 12 and text describe relationships among those spaces. The example says the user-to-device CTM affects the spaces upstream from user space. The previous coordinates_graphics_state_current artifact already tests noncommuting `cm` composition against the page initial CTM (non-origin CropBox, Rotate, UserUnit) and checks Q restoration with both transform and literal output controls. The specialized text/image/form/pattern assertions independently exercise the consumers. This shard avoids duplicating that integration test or claiming that one page certifies every relationship.

ADR review was narrow: ADR0057's form-space pattern conclusion is consistent with §8.3.2.4 together with §8.7.2 and its literal pixel test; ADR0249 records a genuine missing `(initial)` in a quotation, not a changed mapping; ADR0189 explains deterministic f32 geometry but does not create an ISO precision tolerance; ADR0038 was already reviewed in the immediately previous coordinate shard, so it is only cross-referenced.

Verdict: no new behavior failure or test gap worth drafting in this bounded pass. The specific mapping rules have better assertions in their specialized consumer suites than a generic “coordinate system” test would provide. Residuals are the exact f32 precision envelope, 3D projection/support policy, and exhaustive transformations not directly exercised; do not describe these as conformance failures without a more specific source obligation and public consumer.
