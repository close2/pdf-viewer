Bounded review of the three original file-only pointers plus the parent-added `crates/pdf-model/tests/transparency_groups.rs` pointer. Audit was done in the explicit r2038 worktree. This is not full-clause certification.

`crates/test-scenes/src/lib.rs` contains reusable scene builders, not executable assertions. For §11.6.6, the basic transparency and knockout scenes are checked by CPU/GPU and CPU/raster comparisons; those comparisons are valuable backend checks but are not independently derived PDF-specification oracles. The actual PDF fixture suite gives stronger evidence: `a_group_is_composited_once_rather_than_object_by_object` checks a literal overlap pixel and ordinary-form control; `a_group_resets_the_alpha_constants_and_the_blend_mode_for_its_elements` inspects group command state; `a_form_becomes_a_group_only_for_the_transparency_subtype` tests positive and negative subtype controls. Additional tests exercise group color space and knockout semantics with literal pixels. The generated scene for the four-component *page* is §11.4.7, not by itself proof of §11.6.6 isolated group behavior. Renderer-only group-space scenes have dedicated pixel checks; their dynamic expected path reads the scene's own conversion grid, while a separate literal check bounds the principal result.

Primary §11.6.6 is printed pages 421–423 / physical 436–438. It defines only transparency-subtype Form XObjects as groups, resets blend mode/alpha/soft mask for inner execution, sets isolation/knockout backdrop behavior, converts source colors to the group space before compositing, restores state and separately processes each invocation. Table 145's `/CS` remapping text is amended by EC3 #134 to identify the transparency-group XObject's own resource dictionary; `doc/errata-read.md` traces its caret and code path. EC3 #619 carets also on physical page 436 belong to Table 143 / §11.6.5.2 soft-mask image entries, so they do not amend §11.6.6. No whole-clause claim follows from these sampled tests.

I found no direct discriminating assertion for the final repeated-`Do` requirement. The refined draft in `drafts/repeated_group_invocation.rs.txt` explicitly paints white before the first invocation (Screen of 0.5 gray over white stays white), then black over the form, and optionally invokes the group a second time. Its controls independently establish the first white result and the post-black result; the two-call case must produce gray `[128; 3]` inside the form and black outside its BBox. The expected second-call gray is the Screen formula applied to the then-current black backdrop. It detects reusing a previously composited result that captured the first backdrop. It does not condemn caching backdrop-independent content that is recomposited correctly for each call. This remains a proposed assertion gap, not a runtime failure claim. The final-paragraph locator is printed p.423 / physical p.438.

Primary §11.7.2 is printed pages 425–426 / physical 440–441. It requires isolated groups to use their declared space (or the prescribed inherited space where absent), and source colors to convert into the effective group space before blending/compositing. It recommends CIE-based spaces and usually linear gamma, and explicitly says nonlinear spaces still have well-defined results; those recommendations do not support blanket rejection tests. Existing model-PDF witnesses cover the rule better than the scene pointer: `the_blending_space_is_the_one_in_force_rather_than_the_one_declared`, `a_group_that_introduces_a_press_composites_in_it`, `a_colour_from_outside_the_blending_space_is_converted_into_it_and_comes_back`, and calibrated-group counterparts. They combine parsed group structure, reports where no implemented conversion route exists, and literal pixels/independent controls. The group-space scene builders support renderer comparisons but are not PDF-validity tests.

`crates/pdf-model/tests/soft_masks.rs` is predominantly a §11.5 mask derivation suite. Its relevant §11.7 intersections include CMYK mask-group component handling (`a_cmyk_mask_group_takes_its_luminosity_from_the_clauses_own_formula`, `a_cmyk_image_in_a_mask_group_is_decoded_into_the_groups_own_components`, and `a_cmyk_shading_in_a_grey_mask_group_is_sampled_into_the_groups_own_components`), plus the four-component B/overprint-control case. Those use hand-derived channel values, literal pixels, and/or separately painted controls. The test `a_blend_mode_in_a_four_component_mask_group_is_composited_there_and_not_reported` itself is report-only; the adjacent B-fill/stroke case adds pixel evidence for its distinct case. This pointer does not test spot-color fidelity or the broad 11.7.4 mode matrix and should not be counted as complete 11.7 coverage.
