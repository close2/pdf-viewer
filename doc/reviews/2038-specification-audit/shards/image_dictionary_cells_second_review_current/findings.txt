The central source distinction is that §8.9.5.1 has a reader-directed error sentence for inconsistent base-image dictionary entries, while many individual shall-not conditions remain producer rules without a specified reader refusal. EC3 #215 creates a specific ignore behavior: ImageMask ignores its own Mask and ColorSpace, and explicit SMask is ignored when SMaskInData is nonzero. That correction does not erase BPC or Decode constraints. Existing stencil pixels and BPC controls reflect this distinction. The table says unmasked areas are painted using current nonstroking color.

Two already-recorded failures remain current and should not be duplicated. B11 is the correct-length Decode array with a nonnumeric member: the dedicated decode_element_types_audit test fails because parsing filters the bad member rather than reporting the inconsistent image dictionary. The short/long pair-count controls and their positive RGB control are separate. B14 is the sampled valid JPX cdef SMaskInData=2 case: code0 opaque and code1 alpha controls pass, while expected unpremultiplication is refused before rasterization. These statuses come from the global expected-failures inventory; this reviewer did not rerun Cargo.
