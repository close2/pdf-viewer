# Stored annotation appearance blend-mode residual

I checked the effective primary source directly in `ec3-parent-layout.txt`: Table 166 is printed p. 470 / physical p. 485. It says `/BM` is the blend mode used when painting the annotation onto the page, with Normal as the default. The reader paragraph's broad ignore-list text is amended by EC3 #23/#34 to remove `/BM`; #56 adds `/MK`. The composition sentence in §12.5.5 is printed p. 475 / physical p. 490 and also names BM, ca, and CA. The effective amendments are summarized in `doc/errata-read.md` under #23/#34/#56.

The relevant remaining source tension concerns `/ca` and `/CA`: Table 166 says their values are not used when an appearance stream exists, while §12.5.5's composition sentence names all three. This test avoids that dispute: the stored appearance is opaque, its content contains no ExtGState, and the annotation dictionary supplies only `/BM`. Opaque red over opaque blue produces black under Multiply (`[1,0,0] × [0,0,1] = [0,0,0]`) and red under Normal. Table 166 independently specifies absent `/BM` as Normal. A pixel outside the annotation stays blue. These are literal values from the source blend formula, not outputs sampled from the implementation.
