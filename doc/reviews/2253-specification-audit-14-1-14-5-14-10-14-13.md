# Specification audit of ISO 32000-2 §14.1 to §14.5 and §14.10 to §14.13 (procedure sets, metadata, file identifiers, page-piece dictionaries, Web Capture, prepress support, document parts, associated files) — round 2253

The project's twentieth specification audit, made beside the other lanes in the shape of the
owner's review 2038 ([`2038-specification-audit.md`](2038-specification-audit.md)) and of audits
2128 to 2248 ([`2229-specification-audit-14-6-14-9.md`](2229-specification-audit-14-6-14-9.md) is
the clause's middle). Baseline: main at `b9fdeef38`. Evidence:
[`2253-specification-audit-14-1-14-5-14-10-14-13/`](2253-specification-audit-14-1-14-5-14-10-14-13/)
— `findings.json` (2038's schema) and `expected-failures.json` (the failing test of each behaviour
finding). The same entries are in `2038-specification-audit/expected-failures-current.json`.

**This is an audit and fixes nothing.** A fix round takes the findings by the groups at the end.

## Method

1. The `shall`s and `should`s of §14.1 to §14.5 and §14.10 to §14.13
   (`doc/md/ISO_32000-2_sponsored_EC3.md` lines 16213 to 16374 and 17977 to 18830) were read with
   Table 15's `/Info` and `/ID`, Table 21's `/EncryptMetadata`, Table 31's `/LastModified` and
   `/PieceInfo`, and §7.9.4. Clause 14 says of itself that these features "do not affect the final
   appearance of a document", so the lens was the *writers*: §7.5.6's incremental update
   (`pdf_syntax::write`), `pdf-transform`'s `update`, `merge`, `split`, `optimize` and `redact`,
   the rewriting serializer and the linearizer, and the archive converter — every one that writes
   `/Info`, XMP, `/ID` or carries `/PieceInfo`.
2. 2038's findings on these clauses were read first and are not repeated (B19 on boxes outside the
   media box, B33 on a page-level intent without a profile, B36, T14, T16, T54, T144, D21, D25,
   D28, D32, D39). Errata: `doc/errata/site/docs/32000-2-2020/clause14.md` and `doc/errata-read.md`
   — #296 (XMP shall be UTF-8), #328 (the two identifiers no longer "contents"), #69, #691, #482,
   #367, #398, #609 to #611; and two outside the clause that decide findings here: **#251** on
   §7.9.4 (a missing zone "shall be assumed to be the same as Greenwich Mean Time's timezone offset
   (+0'00)", approved — B01) and **#106** on Table 15 (`/Info` no longer "shall be an indirect
   reference", approved — B02, D01).
3. Habits applied: *every reader of a dictionary is a route to it* (five writers read `/Info`, each
   on its own); *an absent entry is a third answer* (a date's unstated zone); *an exception in a
   clause is a route* (Table 21's metadata exemption, decided per object); *a rule about how a
   token is written binds every writer that formats one by hand* (`spelled_date`'s three callers).
4. Each B finding has a failing `*_spec_audit` test whose control passes on main:
   `crates/pdf-transform/tests/metadata_writers_14_3_14_5_spec_audit.rs`, through the public
   `apply` of each verb.

What holds, probed by reading: §14.4 in every writer — an update carries `/ID[0]` and derives a new
`/ID[1]` (`write::identify`), a derived file states one value twice (`serialize::identify`, the
linearizer), the serializer encrypts only at `/R` 6, which takes no identifier into its key;
`SetInformation`'s Table 349 types, `/Trapped` as a name, its refusal to remove `/ModDate` beside a
catalog `/PieceInfo`, and §14.3.1's PDF 2.0 split between dictionary and packet; every XMP writer
refusing a packet that is not UTF-8 (#296) and writing Table 347's two entries; §14.11.2's defaults
(bleed, trim and art from the crop box, each clipped to the media box) and Table 396's "shall be
ignored if no … box is defined" in `guidelines.rs`; §14.11.5's page-level intent ranked over the
catalog's on every route that selects one (`output_intent::selected`, which `content.rs`,
`press.rs` and the reference-XObject frame share); §14.13.2's `/Subtype` and `/Params` on the
converter's associated files, by reading `archive/attach.rs`. §14.2, §14.10, §14.11.4, §14.11.6.1, §14.11.6.3 and §14.11.7 are
`inapplicable` and nothing here contradicts that.

## Findings

Four behaviour gaps, all in writers. In order of how much a person would lose:

- **2253-B03** — redaction leaves a page's private data *current*. `redact`'s `build_page` writes
  the new content and carries every other entry, `/PieceInfo` and `/LastModified` included, so the
  page states a modification time before its last modification and §14.5's equality comparison
  declares the data dictionary to correspond to the current content. The fixture's private data
  holds the redacted text; a producer's (Illustrator's `/Private` is the whole artwork) would too.
- **2253-B04** — under `/EncryptMetadata false`, the packet `SetInformation` creates in an
  encrypted PDF 2.0 file is encrypted: the exemption is asked of the source's catalog, which names
  no packet. The file says its metadata is in the clear and holds ciphertext; the title is lost to
  every reader, this one included.
- **2253-B02** — `optimize`, `redact`, `split` and the archive converter read the trailer's `/Info`
  only as a reference, so a direct one (valid since #106) is dropped; `optimize` and `redact`
  carry the catalog's `/PieceInfo` meanwhile, and the output breaks Table 349's required
  `/ModDate`. The converter's own carve-out date is computed and then never written (by reading).
- **2253-B01** — a §7.9.4 date with no zone is restated into the packet with no zone
  (`spelled_date`): `D:20250102030405` beside `2025-01-02T03:04:05`. Under #251 the first is UTC and
  the second a local time of unknown zone — not "fully equivalent" (§14.3.4), and none of the six
  profiles ADR 1013 says the writer emits. `update`, `merge` and the converter share the function.

Two documentation findings: **D01**, `SetInformation`'s warning tells the operator a direct `/Info`
broke a `shall` #106 struck; **D02**, the 14.3.4 ledger note, `spelled_date`'s doc and ADR 1013
section 5 on the zone, left for B01's fix round.

Not filed, for another lane: `redact` also carries a page's `/Thumb` (§12.3.4), a picture of the
page before the redaction (by reading).

## Ledger rows contradicted

Each stays as it is until the fix round, which re-reads it: `14.3.4` (B01 — its note states the
zoneless spelling as the reading; D02), `14.5` (B03 — "this program writes no data dictionary of
its own, so Table 351's required `/LastModified` binds nothing it writes" is true and misses the
dates it carries), `14.3.3` (B02), `14.3.2` (B04), `7.6.4.2`'s `/EncryptMetadata` reading on the
write side (B04). All are `implemented`.

## Suggested fix groups

1. **Dates into a packet** (`pdf-model/src/xmp.rs`'s `spelled_date`, `pdf-syntax/src/date.rs`) —
   B01 and D02: an unstated zone written as `Z`; the viewer's display decided separately.
2. **The information dictionary in every rewriting writer** (`optimize.rs`, `redact.rs`,
   `split.rs`, `archive/rewrite.rs`, `archive/recovered.rs`, `update.rs`) — B02 and D01: one helper
   that reads `/Info` through `get_key` and carries a direct dictionary.
3. **Private data under a changed content** (`redact.rs`'s `build_page`, the catalog, forms) —
   B03: drop `/PieceInfo` (then `/LastModified`) where the content changed, reported as a loss;
   decided with §12.5.6.23's removal and A834, and `/Thumb` beside it.
4. **Table 21's exemption on the write side** (`pdf-syntax/src/document/encryption.rs`,
   `write.rs`) — B04: decide it against the catalog the update writes.
